About us

History

HistoryQuality

Today, EUROING succeeds in enhancing its reputation for quality in the eyes of its many clients, which is also a form of guarantee of impeccable workmanship and client satisfaction at the same time.

EUROING is currently a company in constant development that aspires to continue growing, as demonstrated by its participation in international tenders, the continuous expansion of its vehicle fleet, and the increase in its number of employees, both highly qualified workers and new professionals who are ready to contribute to the development and modernization of the company.

Our motto is to continuously increase our production capacity, improve our efficiency, and achieve ever better financial results.

Company goals

  • Modernization of production through the use of new environmentally friendly materials;
  • Rational use of equipment, machinery, and workforce;
  • Fast and efficient completion of tasks;
  • Competitive market prices;
  • Modernization of production technology;
  • Constant modernization of equipment and machinery, as well as
  • Further training of technical and administrative staff.
  • Increasing profit

Current projects:

  • Construction of the Gevgelija wastewater treatment plant.

In addition to successful cooperation within our country, EUROING can also take pride in successful cooperation with numerous foreign companies and has achieved many successes beyond the borders of our country.

  • Ukraine, as subcontractors of Granit Skopje
  • The Czech Republic, through the establishment of a joint company for (4) years

Mission:

To work continuously in the Macedonian market and integrate into the international market, develop our own standardized construction projects, create motivating working conditions for our employees and subcontractors, provide economic benefit to all participants in EUROING’s operations, maintain our competitive position in our field in Macedonia, and give investors confidence in us as a trustworthy and reliable partner.

Vision:

To increase the trust of investors and partners through a growing number of satisfied investors, partnerships with leading construction companies, the use of modern technology, work to the highest quality standards, and highly productive machinery.
