Our history

From Gevgelija to international partnerships

EUROING DOO Gevgelija was founded in 1991 as a residential and non-residential construction company. Today, our work covers civil engineering, building construction, hydraulic engineering and renewable energy infrastructure.

Our mission is to deliver quality construction on time through careful planning, skilled execution and open cooperation with investors. We invest in modern equipment, new technologies and the continuous development of our team.

Our vision is sustainable growth in domestic and international markets. We promote energy-efficient design, responsible use of resources and workforce development. Our experience includes cooperation with partners from Greece, Switzerland, Slovenia, Austria, Italy, Ukraine, the USA and the Czech Republic.
